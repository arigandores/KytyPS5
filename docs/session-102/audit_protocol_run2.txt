**Defects**

- **D1 — pred/02 changed the operationalisation, but the ROADMAP was not updated before the first number.**
  - **Evidence:**
    - `docs/ROADMAP.md:1360-1364` (commit `0d35faa`, unchanged at HEAD) still says V2 is a lower bound, so a CLOSE on it is robust.
    - pred/02 §3 (`02_m5_addendum.md:42-43`) withdraws exactly that. It also adds the V2s arm, the X/Y/L logic and the weaker "CLOSE with today's BDA machinery" tier, and rewrites V-e and V-f.
    - pred/01:6 made "recorded in ROADMAP first" part of the protocol. The programme's own precedent (`ROADMAP.md:1324-1325` and `1541-1547`) withdrew session 101's number for skipping that step.
  - **Effect on the result:** none on the branch.
    - pred/01's rule alone also gives CLOSE (X CI_lo 0.412, far above 0.06).
    - Even with pred/01's strict V-e, V2 stays valid. The items that would fail are S1, S4 and S8; their A-arm time is 2797 of 9132 µs, 30.6 %, under the 40 % limit.
  - **What it changes:** the meaning of the CLOSE. The ROADMAP promises a robust lower-bound CLOSE; what was measured is a CLOSE with today's machinery, which pred/02 says the user may reopen. The report must record pred/02 in the ROADMAP, state the late recording as a defect, and must not cite `ROADMAP.md:1363`.
  - **Changes the verdict:** no.

- **D2 — pred/07 describes the retry wrongly: the record thread was not off.**
  - **Evidence** (`log_m5cap102b.txt`):
    - Line 238 reads "KYTY_RECORD_THREAD=0 at start; recordthread= in the gate file moves it".
    - Line 17884 reads "Gate: recordthread=1 frame=1". This pin comes from `gates_base.txt`.
    - Line 17923 reads "record thread on from tick 90", and line 18194 reads "…on from tick 2406" for the second scheduler.
    - The mechanism is documented in `commandRecorder.cpp:1061-1062`.
  - So the retry ran the same record-thread setup as the crashed run, except for ticks 1–89. The claim at pred/07:14-19 that the record thread was off before the capture is false. The retry's success is not evidence that `KYTY_RECORD_THREAD=0` avoids the crash; the crash stays undiagnosed.
  - **Effect on the capture:** none.
    - Both schedulers went "record thread off" at the capture request (lines 514799 and 514882), before "capture started" at 514883.
    - They came back on only after "capture finished" at 516189 (lines 516199 and 516209), which agrees with `commandRecorder.cpp:1081`.
    - pred/01 §4 allows a technical retry without requiring any change, so the retry is still valid.
  - **Changes the verdict:** no. The report must say this and must not describe the capture as taken without the record thread.
