1. **MAJOR — the size −418.7 µs is inflated by the estimator's window.** The sealed estimator keeps frames 60–88 of
   each 90-frame block. Against the full block its gap is within ±1.6 SE in five earlier runs but −186 µs (z −3.09) in

   → FACTS, ROADMAP items 6–7, the `gates.cpp` comment corrected; future seals pre-register the full block (or frames
   10–89) as the main estimator (decision after session 110).
2. **MAJOR — the D/M duration rule is weak.** M_A, M_B and most of D are one startup race on shader 497 at guest frame 3
   (if the lookahead walk queues its prefetch first, the dispatch waits about that walk's length, 12.1–14.2 ms against
   walks of 12.5–15.1 ms, 7/7; else it compiles synchronously in ~0.95 ms), independent of the arm (A 4/8, B 3/8
   entries). Resampling the 16 entries, the rule fails 12 % of the time with no effect and would let post-load stall
   time grow 4–12× before failing. The conclusion still stands: after load arm B adds +0.33 ms per 150 s entry; with
   the compute precache on (shipping) there are 0 stalls. → recorded; a future duration guard excludes the startup
   interval or bounds the post-load part separately.
