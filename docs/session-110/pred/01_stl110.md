## 0. Why

Session 109: `cspfree=1` verifies clean (`vfy109`: 0 bad in 2.06 M), removes every prefetch acquisition of
`PipelineCache::m_mutex` and shortens the mean frame by 154 µs at the pin (`frm109`, a measurement), but with the
compute precache off it showed 17 vs 7 dispatch-time stall EVENTS (`ent109b`; waits for still-compiling pipelines 12
vs 3; one-sided p ≈ 0.03). The count does not measure how long a player waits. This run measures the stall DURATION.

## 1. What is tested

Build `b3f7a2c957dd344bfd140fe6d04eb2b95ac9387d01dabf5b7dde42b6765b6a82` (`3d8af4c`: `cspfree` from `5770106` plus the
stall-duration instrument): every dispatch-time stall — a synchronous compute compile in `GetComputePipeline`
(from the "not found" branch, lock held, to the return) or a wait for a pending entry (from the start of the wait to
the wake) — logs `CsStall: kind=new|wait us=<N> id=<id> hash=0x<hash>` (always on, ≤ 4 096 lines) and adds to
`cs_sync_new_us` / `cs_sync_wait_us` on `FrameTrace-x`. Arm A `gates_free0.txt` (`cspfree=0`), arm B
`gates_free1.txt` (`cspfree=1`).

## 3. Admission (each term has its own failing fixture)

As `ent109b`: `DRAFT`/`SEAL`, `IDENTITY`, `ORDER` (increasing, distinct launch stamps), `INPUTS`; per entry `BINARY`,
`PREREG`, `ENV_PRECACHE`, `ENV_PIN`, `ENV_FORBIDDEN`, `GATES_ARM` (the arm's token at the tail, the other absent),
`ATTEMPT` (one ok attempt, no hold exit, ≥ 142.5 s), `PIN_ONCE` (exactly one `GpuClockPin:` line and it is `mode 1`),
`PRECACHE_OFF` (the first `PipelinePrecache` line queued 0 compute pipelines), `NO_MARKER` (the `run_safety99` tokens
plus `--- Error ---` and `AsyncPipelines: skipped draw`, in the log AND in `stdout_<tag>.txt`), `ROWS` (≥ 1 000
`FrameTrace-x` rows each with every counter incl. `cs_sync_new_us`/`cs_sync_wait_us`), `ARM_A_DARK`, `ARM_B_ARMED`;
plus **`STALL_SYNC`**: the entry's `CsStall` lines equal its Σ(`cs_sync_new` + `cs_sync_wait`), allowing one stall in
flight at exit (0 ≤ counters − lines ≤ 1).

## 4. Rule

Per entry D = Σ `us` of its `CsStall` lines, M = the longest; D_A, D_B sum over each arm's four entries; M_A, M_B the
longest over them. **NO_POWER** if arm A has no `CsStall` line. **PASS** if D_B ≤ 1.25 · D_A + 20 000 µs AND M_B ≤ M_A +
10 000 µs. **FAIL** otherwise (then `cspfree` is not shipped; ROADMAP first). On PASS: the ship ABBA (`pred/02_shp110.md`,
its own seal) before any default change.

## 5. Predictions (published whether hit or miss)

L1 NO_POWER does not happen (arm A has ≥ 1 stall) · L2 counts again higher in B (S_B > S_A) · L3 PASS (medium
confidence) · L4 M_A and M_B each < 50 000 µs (stalls from a warm driver cache are short) · L5 D_B / D_A in [0.8, 2.5].
