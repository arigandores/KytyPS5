## Admission, verdicts and consequences

Exactly as the scorer's docstring and ROADMAP 118 items 2 and 4: admission (build, pin, exact environment, hold, one
attempt, this pre-registration, gate text, every `Gate:` line a schedule flip at a block start with its arm's value,
well-formed ABBA `GateArm:` lines, one `Spine:` line, no failure marker, ≥ 20 complete blocks of each arm, both arms
armed, `spine_el`/ops in [0,95; 1,05], idle GPU); estimator window frames 10–88 of each block.
- **K5** (every `FrameTrace-x` line after 1800): FAIL on any `spine_bad`/`spine_misal`/`cram_write` or mismatch line;
  PASS at `spine_cmp` ≥ 90 % of `spine_el` (arm P kept); else NOT_EVALUABLE.
- **C** (carry): FAIL on any `carry_bad` or `SpineCarry:` line; PASS at `carry_cmp` ≥ 90 % of plans with a complete
  predecessor (`spine_n − carry_skip`); else NOT_EVALUABLE. **Safe plan:** `spine_unc` + `spine_abort` ≤ 10 % of plans,
  else K5/C NOT_EVALUABLE — a FAIL of K5 or C is stronger than this condition.
- **K3:** median largest-run share ≥ 0,25 ⇒ W ≤ 4, else W up to 8 (a bound).
- **K4:** median of the write-crossing share `dep` ≤ 300 ‰ ⇒ PASS, separately at W = 2 and W = 4; evaluable only when
  ≥ 50 % of the arm-P kept frames carry exactly one finished frame (`sc_frames` = 1) and `k4_wW_n` = 1.

| outcome | consequence |
|---|---|
| NOT_ADMITTED | one repeat `go118a.sh spk118r`; a second ⇒ part 2 recorded as not measured, next step decided in ROADMAP |
| K5 or C FAIL | **STOP_STAGE4** until the hazard the diagnostic lines name has a design |
| K5, C or K4 W = 2 NOT_EVALUABLE | one repeat `spk118r` after the cause is recorded |
| K4 W = 2 FAIL | **CLOSE_A** — route A closed for maximum FPS |
| K4 W = 2 PASS, W = 4 FAIL / NOT_EVALUABLE | **W2_CEILING** — W = 2 is the route's ceiling; its best case recomputed with f = 0,5 before stage 3 resumes |
| all PASS | **STAGE3** — stage 3 resumes (M3.2 …), stage 5 (W = 2) the first milestone with a gain |

The rows are applied top to bottom: STOP_STAGE4 and NOT_EVALUABLE precede CLOSE_A. Arm M decides nothing: its levels
feed a removability design for any candidate ≥ 1 ms, recorded before code. Report only (item 4): at W = 2 and 4 the share
of qualifying frames above the bar and the median per arm-P block; `carry_skip` as a share of the plans.

## Limits

One entry, one launch state (BDA regime reported); the census and mode 2 cost milliseconds a frame (arm-P levels are not
speed levels; the census's own time `sc_ns`); the carry is tested on the GuestGpu thread between consecutive plans of a
processor (a walker-thread spine would also need its own chain and the same submission-level reset); `dep` counts an image
written in one segment and used in the adjacent one — images only read by both are not counted, images written through
buffers (not image bindings) are invisible. Residual risks of the safe plan (item 4, m6): words the GPU writes earlier in
the same submission are not seen by `IsGpuClean` at plan time; the exact header checks of `INDEX_TYPE`,
`INDEX_BUFFER_SIZE`, `INDEX_BASE` are not repeated; value-level `EXIT_NOT_IMPLEMENTED` in register handlers stays a
residual risk. About 4,5 % of `FrameTrace-x` rows carry the census of two frames or none (item 4, m7) — the `== 1` filter
drops them, equally in both scene modes.
