**Defects (none changes the verdict):**

1. **GPU Duration is the time until an event finishes after the work before it, not the event's own cost.** Of the per-event readings in the PS items, 30–54 % are exactly 0.0, spread across 530 of S3's 600 events and similar for the other PS items (113,402 zero readings over 100 fetches). The CS items have none. When item draws slow down, they absorb time previously shown on neighbouring events.

   - Frame-total estimator: X (all 10 items) 1.3805, CI [0.3716, 0.3842], against 1.4073 item-based. Y 1.3256, CI [0.3143, 0.3627], about the same.
   - L drops to 1.0095 and V2s/A to 1.0280 (CI [−0.004, 0.059]). The published V2s/A 1.0608 and L 1.0208 are therefore about twice the frame cost.
   - Effect on the decision: none. X and Y still cross 6 % and L still does not, so the tier stays "machinery".
2. **The replacement itself adds time outside the items.** B's frame total is 272 µs above A's (frame-based B/A about 1.03) while the item sums match. V-d only looks at item sums, so it cannot see this. Not decisive.
3. **The sealed 5-arm design does not balance carry-over.** Within a round, A never follows V2 or V2s, and V2 always follows V1 or V2s. pred/01's "Williams design, carry-over balanced" reasoning therefore no longer holds after addendum 02 §4. Measured carry-over by predecessor is at most about 1.2 % on arm sums, so it is negligible here.
4. **GPU clocks were not locked:** SM clock ranged 1,537–2,640 MHz in the 5 s samples. Round 2 is an outlier (S[A] 10,310 against about 9,120; X 1.3148; L 0.9228). The medians absorb it.
