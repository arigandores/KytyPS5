## Evaluability (else NOT_EVALUABLE — the scene enters the map unjudged, never "no effect")

- **≥ 24 pairs AFTER the outlier drops** (item 8 (c), 17 (c)). Pairs = blocks (2k, 2k+1), both complete, both with

- 2SE defined (≥ 2 pairs); both arms in the same BDA regime (`bda_scan` ≤ 300 NEW, ≥ 600 OLD; otherwise MIXED —

## The rule

- Per pair: B block mean − A block mean of `dt_us` over the estimator rows. **Δ = mean over pairs; 2SE =
  2·stdev(per-pair differences)/√pairs; slope s = Δ / 2 000 µs, its 2SE likewise.**
- **Verdict (item 2, RC4–RC5), in this order:** SUPERLINEAR `s − 2SE > 1`; CRITICAL `s − 2SE > 0,5` and `s + 2SE ≥ 1`;
  NOT_CRITICAL `s + 2SE < 0,3`; otherwise NOT_RESOLVED ("НЕ РАЗРЕШЕНО").
- **Draw-adjusted estimator beside the raw one (report only):** per pair B mean `dt` − A mean `dt` × (B mean `draws` /
  A mean `draws`); its Δ, 2SE, slope and zone are printed; a zone that differs from the raw zone prints
  `DRAW-ADJUSTED ZONE DIFFERS`. The verdict is the raw slope's (RC4 is written on it).
- Scene classification (live `lite` counters, estimator rows of the paired blocks): medians of `gpu_busy/dt`,
  `cpu_gpu/dt`, `cpu_main/dt`, mean `rec_spin_us`, the vblank multiples of `dt` with their shares, **PINCHED** when ≥ 80 %
  of the frames sit on one multiple (the shares of every step are printed beside it — a saving smaller than the distance
  to the next step does not move FPS), **GPU-bound** when median `gpu_busy/dt` ≥ 0,9 and the GuestGpu arm (code 1) is
  NOT_CRITICAL; any other code prints `n/a`.
- **Reading (items 17 (d), 18 (c)) — by the measured property of THIS run, never by the level name:** over the window
  rows (positions 10–88) of the base-arm (`burn=0`) blocks of the population pairs (after the outlier drops), the share
  of frames with `dt_us` **< 25 000** (midway between one and two vblanks). Share **≥ 0,95** ⇒ `reading=HEADROOM`: the
  slope measures the work the frame absorbs before it falls to the next vblank step, the game-speed line is withheld and
  the number is never quoted as speed. No base-arm window row in the population ⇒ `reading=NOT_EVALUABLE` (never
  HEADROOM), the game-speed line withheld as well. Otherwise `reading=SPEED` and game speed per arm = 16 667 / mean
  `dt_us`. The share, its frame count and the threshold are printed on every report.
- **BDA regime (item 17 (e)):** every verdict line carries the run's regime; a scene in OLD (expected: the ghost world,
  `bda_scan` ≈ 1 113) is quoted only with its regime and never compared with NEW scenes.
- `cpu/wall` of the dose is printed against ≈ 1,00 (block codes) — diagnostics, never a verdict.

## Consequences

- **CRITICAL / SUPERLINEAR:** the burnt thread is on the scene's critical path; its track is the scene's candidate
  lever (SUPERLINEAR is read with the step shares, never 1:1). On a HEADROOM reading the same zone names the thread
  that eats the scene's headroom, not a speed lever.
- **NOT_CRITICAL:** no track of that thread is opened by the scene; with GPU-bound, the scene opens the shader track.
- **NOT_RESOLVED / NOT_EVALUABLE:** the scene enters the map without a slope verdict; no lever is opened or closed by it.
- **NOT_ADMITTED:** one repeat `<tag>r`; a repeat that fails again is NOT_EVALUABLE. Host-event and outlier-pair
  invalidations are repeated whole.
- The main guest thread is not measured by this map (item 13 (b)); the direction of session 123 is then decided by the
  GuestGpu slope and the vblank step shares. Code 4 stays a debt of the session: "the site does not fire; cause not
  diagnosed"; code 7 is recorded beside it: "arms, but on a thread other than the main guest thread".
