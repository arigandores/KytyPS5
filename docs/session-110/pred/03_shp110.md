## 0. Why

`cspfree=1` removes every prefetch acquisition of `PipelineCache::m_mutex` on the walker (`frm109`: `cspf_have` 0,
`cspfree_hit` 266 a flip), verifies against the locked path (`vfy109`: 0 bad in 2.06 M) and adds no dispatch-time
stall duration with the compute precache off (`stl110b`: D 10.9 vs 19.6 ms, longest 1.6 vs 12.3 ms). A measurement of
the same ABBA (`frm109`, pred/04 of session 109, nothing shipped) read Δ mean `dt` −154.1 µs (2·SE 121.2). This is the
run that decides the default.
