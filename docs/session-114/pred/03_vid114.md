## 4. Known limits (must not be claimed)

- The detector has **no positive control**: the gating of item 4 cannot be switched off, so a run where two threads
  present at once was never produced; the detector is an atomic counter reviewed by reading, its zone per item 11.
- **Audit M1:** `present_overlap` is counted only from the guest's first flip on (earlier counts become the baseline of
  `FlipQueue::Flip`), so in the boot window the `PresentOverlap:` log line (≤ 8 lines) is the only detector.
- `title_default_on` is a mean with ≈ 2× headroom: one UpdateTitle stall ≥ ~110 ms in 120 s would read FAIL (accepted as
  conservative; the row median is reported).
- The boot stress covers the startup park of one boot; a race that needs another interleaving is not excluded.
- The video is one 120-s scene; a PASS says nothing about other scenes.
- No speed is measured here; the size of seal 02b is the only one quoted, with its regime.
